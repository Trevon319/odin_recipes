ICE BOX SANDWICH

Yummy and easy to make Ice cream sandwich

Ingrediants

  • 1 package (3.30z) instant vanilla pudding mix
  • 2 cups cold 2% milk
  • 2 cups whipped topping
  • 1 cup miniature semisweet chockalte chips
  • 24 whole graham crackers halved

Steps

  1. Mix pudding and milk according to package directions; regrigerate until set. Fold in whipped topping and chochalate chips
  2. Place 24 graham cracker halves on a baking sheet; top each with about 3 tablespoons filling. Place other graham cracker half on top
  3. Wrap individually in waxed paper; freeze until firm(1 hour)
  4. Serve sandwiches
